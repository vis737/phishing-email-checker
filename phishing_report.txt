[CHECKLIST]  PHISHING RED-FLAG CHECKLIST

   1. Urgent / threatening language
      Words like 'immediately', 'account suspended', 'act now', 'within 24 hours'
   2. Mismatched / shortened URLs
      Links that don't match the display text or use bit.ly/tinyurl obfuscation
   3. Unknown or spoofed sender
      Address doesn't match the supposed organisation (e.g. support@paypa1.com)
   4. Requests for sensitive data
      Asks for passwords, credit-card numbers, SSNs, or login credentials
   5. Generic greeting
      Dear Customer / Dear User instead of your real name
   6. Spelling & grammar errors
      Professional organisations rarely send emails full of typos
   7. Unexpected attachments
      .exe, .zip, .scr or macro-enabled Office files
   8. Too-good-to-be-true offers
      You've won a prize you never entered for
   9. Mismatched display name vs address
      Display says 'Bank of America' but address is random@gmail.com
  10. Suspicious reply-to address
      Reply-To differs from the From address

[KEY TAKEAWAYS]

  * Never click links in suspicious emails -- hover to preview the URL first.
  * Check the sender's actual email address, not just the display name.
  * Legitimate organisations will never ask for passwords or PINs via email.
  * If something feels urgent or too good to be true, verify independently.
  * Report phishing emails to your IT department or reportphishing@apwg.org.
